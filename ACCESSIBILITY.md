# Accessibility Statement for VEX

The VEX engineering team is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all developers, regardless of ability or assistive technology.

---

## ♿ Conformance Status
The VEX Studio Suite aims to adhere to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA** standards.

---

## 🛠️ Measures to Support Accessibility
- **Semantic HTML5**: Native buttons, header landmarks, and modal dialogs with focus trapping and ARIA labels.
- **High Contrast Ratios**: Dark integration cards and high-contrast typography designed for readability across mobile and desktop displays.
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Full keyboard tab-indexing for modal dialogs, register inspectors, and prompt editors.
- **Screen Reader Support**: Descriptive labels for SVG icons, connection status badges, and dynamic status dots.
- **Responsive Touch Targets**: Minimum 44x44px touch targets on mobile viewports for header buttons, drawer navigation, and copy actions.
